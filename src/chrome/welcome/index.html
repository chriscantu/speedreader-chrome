SpeedReader

Read one word at a time, at your own pace.

SpeedReader uses Rapid Serial Visual Presentation (RSVP) to flash one word at a time in a fixed location, so your eyes stay still and your brain catches up. Pick a comfortable speed on the next screen — you can change it any time from Options.

Pick your speed

Press Start preview to feel the cadence, then drag the slider until it feels right. The preview pauses on its own after two passes.
